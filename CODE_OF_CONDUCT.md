# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical decisions on their
merits, welcome questions, and give specific, constructive feedback. Respect
people's names, identities, privacy, and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated
personal attacks are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, or other
project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using a contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish another person's private
information in an issue. Maintainers will review reports and limit disclosure
to what is needed to investigate and respond. A maintainer involved in a report
should not decide its outcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, or
restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity, context, and
history of the conduct. These expectations also apply to maintainers.
